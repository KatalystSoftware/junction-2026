# Kutsu

**What it is.** A City of Espoo talent-services officer is preparing employers for a recruitment
event (the Deep Tech Talent Forum, 26–27 Nov 2026, 27 employers). For each employer the tool drafts,
from public sources, what kind of talent the company needs. The officer checks and edits the draft,
sends it to the company, and the company confirms or corrects it. Only a confirmed brief is meant to
reach talent.

**Who uses it.** First the officer: at a desk, in daylight, working through a list of employers, a
few minutes each. Second the company contact: opens one link, once, probably on a phone, and wants
to say "yes" or fix a line in under a minute.

**What the officer must be able to do in seconds.** See which company this is. Read its needs. See
the sentence behind any claim. Change a word. Send it. See what came back.

**Product truth that design must not blur.**
- Needs are inferred. Statements are quoted. The two are never presented as the same kind of thing.
- Every quoted statement was found word for word on the page it links to; statements that failed
  that check, or that named a person, were dropped.
- A brief is a draft until the company confirms it, and the screen always says which it is.
- Some companies have almost no public record. The screen says so instead of padding.

**Scope.** A feature demo: one working screen and the company's reply screen. No accounts, no
settings, no dashboard.

**Voice.** Plain, short, specific. Controls name their action ("Send to Vexlum").
