# Surface brief: the company's sign-up (#/join/<id>)

Mode: Persuade, inside the world already built (the company's own colour and logo, white sheets for
answers, Hanken Grotesk). Audience: a company's contact, invited by Espoo, deciding in a minute
whether this is worth their time. Proof: their own logo, their own news with sources, roles already
drafted. Must stay: every prefilled value can be corrected; sources are one hover away.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One card at a time, and your page builds around it. The centre holds the single thing to
decide. What you accept flies to the side and stays in view: who you are on the left, who you want
to meet on the right. Refused: the form wizard that hides everything behind steps and a step bar.

OWN-WORLD: The company's colour fills the screen. One white card in the centre is the only bright
surface. The sides are written directly on the colour in the company's ink, no boxes, so the centre
keeps the eye. No subtitles under headings, no underlined links: sources are chips that open the
quoted sentence.

STORY: "That's us." Then four pieces of their own news, keep or leave out. Then each drafted role,
accept, change or remove. Then anyone else, from one line. Then sign up, with the whole page already
standing around the button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Invitation first. Then three columns: left about 20rem, centre card about 38rem,
right about 20rem. Under the card: a row of dots for progress and the keys (Enter, Backspace, left
arrow). On a phone the card comes first and the two lists stack under it.

Signature interaction: accepting a card morphs it into its collapsed line at the side; clicking that
line morphs it back into the card.

FORM: pinned by the user. seed: none (pinned) — "slide them on the side collapsed, summarized, so
you always see the full picture ... it's kind of building dynamically your page ... if you click it,
it transitions back to editing it, and on hover it might show some more details ... left or right
sidebar with the full context and the center is your editor ... the candidates looking for is on the
right, the background on the left", "keyboard shortcut hint spacebar or enter", "remove the slop
subtitles! underlines", "not be in a tabular format, but more a card".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
