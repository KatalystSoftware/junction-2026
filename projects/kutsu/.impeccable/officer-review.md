# Surface brief: the officer's review screen (and the company's reply screen)

Mode: Operate, with a showpiece first viewport because it is shown as a demo.
Audience and job: see PRODUCT.md. Proof: real logos, real quotes, real links.
Must stay: the quote check, the stated/inferred split, the live search for any company.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page becomes the company. The employer's own logo and colour take over the screen, and
what the officer came for, what this company needs, is the largest type on it. Refused: the grey
admin table of company rows with a detail drawer, and the report page of stacked text cards.

OWN-WORLD: Two brands meet. The rail is the city's: one deep blue field, white type, employers as
logo tiles. The stage is the company's: a logo plate at poster scale beside a full-bleed field in
the company's most chromatic brand colour, ink chosen for contrast. Below it a white working ground:
needs as large display lines with numbered superscript citations, evidence as a dense ruled ledger,
a three-stop approval track. Bricolage Grotesque for display, Hanken Grotesk for text.

STORY: The officer sees who it is, reads three needs, hovers a number and sees the sentence behind
it, fixes a word, sends it. The company confirms. The track completes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail left, 300px, employers grouped by status. Stage right: logo plate and brand
field across the top (about 40% of the height) with name, slogan and four facts; the approval track
with its one primary action at the right of the field; the needs heading and the first needs inside
the first viewport.

Signature interaction: choosing a company wipes its colour across the stage from the logo, and the
needs arrive in sequence. Hovering a citation number opens the source: quote, title, site, date.

FORM: pinned by the user. seed: none (pinned) — "make the company logo the main thing, make the
page more company branded, pick some colors from their logos", "really flashy really hype really
dynamic", "make the citations links that open up or show on hover the source wikipedia like",
"show the approval process, they can edit it, send it to the company to approve".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the
verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
