Recruitment is hard.
Kutsu makes it easier.

Kutsu takes the setup work off the City of Espoo’s recruitment events, so the city can hold many small, well-matched ones and spend its time on the people.

City of Espoo · Rethinking Recruitment · Junction 2026

Why now

Matching by CV skills is solved.
Motivation and fit aren’t.

Espoo’s organisers name three things a good match needs: skill, motivation and fit. AI matches the first from a CV. Only people find the other two.

Problem

Matching is hard, because it takes people connecting

Matching by CV skills is easy now. Real matching, on motivation and fit, needs the right people in the same room. Every meeting costs time, and today who meets whom is arranged by hand, from too little information.

Companies

Say what they need too late

…they provide this information to the event organizer too late, only just before the event day.
Job seekers

Can’t tell which companies are worth meeting

The company list and what each wants arrive late, if at all.

Organisers

Pair people by hand

They read every application themselves, often without the tech background to judge it.

Every event

Starts from zero

The sign-up survey and both invitation lists are built again, by hand, for each event.

Problem

Big events are bloated. Small events are laborious.

Big event

Hundreds of people, dozens of stands

  • Employers are overwhelmed by large volumes of largely irrelevant candidate approaches.
  • Staff spend a day at a stand to find a few relevant people.
  • Job seekers queue at the well-known names and miss the rest.
Small event

One theme, a few employers, the right people

  • Almost every conversation is relevant, on both sides.
  • But each takes the same setup as a big one: find the companies, find the people, match them.
  • So the city can afford only a few.

The format that works best is the one that is hardest to run.

Solution

Kutsu: less time on setup, more time with people

AI drafts, people confirm: companies and job seekers each check every line and say what they want. Kutsu does the rest of the setup, so Espoo can hold many more small events.

Solution

Two pools break the chicken-and-egg problem

Companies ask which candidates will be there before they commit. People sign up once they know which companies come. A one-off event starts that wait from zero, every time.
