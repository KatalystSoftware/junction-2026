# Assumptions to check with the Espoo mentors

The prototype is built on these assumptions. Each one changes the design if it is wrong.

1. **Do organisers decide who is introduced to whom, or who is admitted to the event?** We designed for introductions: everyone registered can attend, and organisers plan conversations. Admission screening would be a much heavier decision about individuals, with higher AI Act and fairness stakes. Is any admission screening done today, and should the tool support it?
2. **Which registration tool is used today** (Tavata, Brella or an own form)? Can we get an export or an API, so Esittely sits on top of it instead of replacing it?
3. **Job ads as the source of the company draft.** May we draft from companies' public job ads (their own site, Duunitori, The Hub, Job Market Finland), or does each company need to agree first?
4. **Scale and timing.** How many companies, candidates and organisers does a typical event have? How many weeks before the event do companies sign up?
5. **"Finnish required".** How often is this a habit rather than a real need? Would companies accept being asked about it directly?
6. **Consent and visibility.** When may a company see a candidate's name: after the organiser's introduction, or only at the event with the candidate's agreement?
7. **Legal basis and retention.** Can the core questionnaire rely on the statutory employment-service task, with consent only for the optional parts? How long may data be kept after the event? Does the city's legal team treat any recruitment-related AI as high-risk under AI Act Annex III(4) regardless of design?
8. **Who are the organisers?** Are the employer side (Business Espoo) and the candidate side (International Working Life Services) different teams? Who should see the fairness check?
9. **Online and hybrid events.** Does "stand" translate to online booths or booked time slots?
10. **Follow-up.** Can both sides be contacted after the event for the one-tap relevance question?
11. **Language of the service.** Finnish, Swedish and English at minimum? Should candidate answers be translated for organisers?
12. **Success metric for a pilot.** Is "share of planned conversations rated relevant by both sides" the right headline number for Espoo?
